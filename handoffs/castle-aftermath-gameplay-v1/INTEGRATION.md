# Live adapter contracts and animation staging

All events run on the gameplay authority/main thread. Outputs are component state, not dispatchers. Read `Accepted` immediately after the corresponding command; it is reset at the beginning of that command. Persist stable order/body/gate IDs; never treat actor pointers as durable identity. No action is completed merely because a timer elapsed.

## Workshop, placement and convoy (#50)

1. The existing shop owns gold. Select a placement ghost first. Check ground/slope, full cart clearance, route, queen-realm exclusion, destination footprint, two available mechanics and funds. Reserve a stable order ID. Revalidate immediately before charging. Charge once through the existing economy, then call `BeginOrder(OrderId, PaymentCommitted, PlacementValid)` on a **new component per order**. Only construct the cart after acceptance; transaction recovery/refund belongs to the shop adapter. This controller must not become a second gold balance.
2. Use laptop2 meshes and their supplied pivots. UE centimeters: WheelL `(0,-62,55)`, WheelR `(0,62,55)`, radius `55.4`; barrel cradle `(0,0,75)`; pull handle `(220,0,57)`; push handle `(-105,0,90)`. Check whether imported vertices already include offsets before setting component transforms; do not apply offsets twice.
3. One authoritative cart route owns displacement. Crew follows projected front/rear handle goals, with hand IK on the handles and walking speed matched to measured movement. Do not average two independent AI MoveTo positions to move the cart. Default maximum sampled travel is 180 cm/s, a deliberate heavy-work pace; retune after stride/contact tests. Never stretch a walk clip far beyond the office's ±15–20% rule.
4. Each update sets crew alive, cart alive, route validity, both crew-at-handle booleans, danger/action interruption, destination arrival and destination still clear. `MoveAllowed` drives the cart. It stops while assembling/paused/deploying. Dead crew or destroyed cart terminates the order; do not teleport a replacement into place. Surviving mechanics retreat through their existing combat/navigation logic.
5. Measure each wheel contact's **signed incremental** displacement projected on the midpoint forward tangent of that wheel's route. Call `SampleWheelTravel(dt, leftCm, rightCm, teleported)`. Set each wheel's local-Y rotation to its output phase relative to its captured bind rotation. Do not AddRotation using total accumulated distance. Reverse produces reverse rotation; the outside wheel travels farther in turns. Refresh previous contact positions after a teleport or rejected hitch; never accumulate rejected distance into the next sample. Samples over 0.25 s or above the speed allowance are rejected.
6. `ThreatActive` publishes the convoy objective to the queen's defenders. `EvaluateQueenThreat` returns true for an active convoy when a capable queen defender can reach it. The existing siege/AI target selector must prefer that objective over ordinary travel/castle targets, while death/stun and committed attack recovery still control the actor. If a route is blocked, attack the blocking obstacle or replan; never walk through walls. This is separate from #48 hearing. This package does not yet rewire BP_Zombie's target selection.
7. On `DeployRequested`, reserve that OrderId in the adapter's pending-spawn registry, recheck destination and create one BP_Cannon. A duplicate callback must look up the same cannon. Call `ConfirmDeployment(OrderId, true)` only for a valid placed cannon; false means no cannon was created. Hide/remove the barrel on the cart only after acceptance. Return the empty cart and mechanics along their route. On load, reconcile OrderId against already placed cannons **before** permitting another spawn.

## Gate pieces, repair and memory (#162 / #50)

Put `BPC_GateAftermath162` on a persistent gate controller, not on a destructible leaf that is destroyed. Keep it across waves. Bind each destruction cycle's stable debris IDs `0..Pieces-1` (maximum 64 gameplay pieces; merge small cosmetic chips into nonblocking clusters).

- At the actual intact-to-broken transition call `RecordBlast(newRevision, pieces)`. Repeated blast notifications while already broken do not duplicate rubble or scars. Freeze settled debris into cheap collision/render representations through the existing gate adapter. Keep the recognizable large planks and metal straps; tiny chips should not form invisible nav blockers.
- `StartWork(newJobId, OnlyClearPassage)` begins inspection. The shop/dispatcher verifies payment and mechanic availability first. A job ID may only be accepted once. A clear-only order never rebuilds the gate.
- Stage 1: 2 seconds inspecting. Mechanic kneels/points at the broken hinge, glances toward the other mechanic; no HP change.
- Stage 2: clear one real piece at a time. `ClaimPiece(revision,id)` reserves a piece. Approach its grip, crouch, grasp on an animation notify, stand/drag or carry, then lay it in the nearby salvage pile. Heavy beams use both mechanics; small planks may use one. Piece remains visible while carried. Call `ConfirmPieceCleared` only after it is at salvage **and** its passage collision/nav obstruction has actually been removed. Each piece credits salvage once. `ReleasePiece` releases a failed/interrupted claim without removing or crediting the piece. A carried piece interrupted by combat is safely dropped and later rebound by ID.
- Stage 3: 3 seconds bracing the frame. One mechanic holds a board/jack and the other aligns it. Stage 4: 8 seconds assembling, with `BuildProgress` revealing new boards/bands through authored attachment stages. Stage 5: 2 seconds checking the hinge/fastening. Timber impacts produce chips/dust; sparks only for plausible metal contact. Tool impacts and hand poses come from animation notifies, not a free-running unrelated effect.
- `AdvanceWork(dt, CrewAtWorkPoint, HandsAvailable, ThreatInterrupts)` accepts steps up to 0.25 s. Threat or missing crew pauses progress and work animation. Resume the same stage after reaching a safe work position. Do not fast-forward dropped hitch time.
- Stage 6 sets `ReadyToRestore`. The live adapter restores actual gate geometry/HP via its current contract, keeps the passage open, checks for overlapping units and rebuilds collision/nav safely. It then calls `ConfirmRestored(jobId, PhysicalGateReady, PassageSafe)`. Only accepted confirmation marks the gate repaired and adds a repair band. This component has no independent gate HP pool and never closes the gate on passing units.
- Stage 7 is complete. Show a short shoulder wipe/relief glance via #163; return to workshop. Scars, salvage history and repair bands survive this completion and later waves. A second destruction increments scars without erasing previous repairs.

Across restart, map primitive fields from `battle_memory.py` into the existing SaveGame after #166 export. Stable world/campaign + gate IDs are required. Save debris state and settled transforms, repair stage and progress, scars and repair bands. On restore, recreate visible scars and salvage before enabling navigation; release transient hand/socket claims and rebind by ID. The supplied codec validates round trips, but no game save slot is currently written by this package.

## Continuous blob morph (#164)

Use one corresponding-topology mesh throughout. The neutral basis is the final zombie; `Blob` and `Puddle` are sculpted targets on the **same vertices/index buffer**, not unrelated meshes. The current assets/targets are requested in #165. Do not fake this by scaling a blob down, waiting, spawning an egg, and swapping to a zombie.

`BeginMorph(id)` starts with 4% of the zombie basis already present. Over 1.6 seconds, Melt rises smoothly from 0 to 1 while Form simultaneously rises toward 1 over 4.2 seconds. Drive the Blob/Puddle target weights with `BlobWeight`/`PuddleWeight`; their sum plus Form is always 1. Let head/shoulder/hand shapes read from the start, still soft and unfinished. Add coherent traveling wobble with the reference in `continuous_morph.hlsl`; pin soles/ground contact, use a per-spawn seed, and increase mesh bounds appropriately after measuring displacement.

`SampleAge(real elapsed spawn time)` is monotonic and cannot rewind. At 4.2 s it requests handoff. Keep the forming mesh visible until the final zombie is confirmed visible, positioned and ready. `ConfirmHandoff(id, ZombieVisibleAndReady)` is exactly once. Prefer the same skeletal mesh/actor becoming active, avoiding a visual swap. If a separate actor is necessary, reconcile its stable SpawnId before spawn and match transform/pose before replacing presentation. Death during formation calls `CancelMorph`; no later callback may create a zombie.

The HLSL and CPU reference are a deformation contract, not a compiled material or authored targets. The final effect must be judged in Unreal at normal gameplay distance and slow motion.

## Small human reactions (#163)

Kinds: 0 danger glance, 1 flinch/fear, 2 crew coordination gesture, 3 relief. Durations are 0.6 s (fear 0.8); per-kind cooldowns 8/12/6/15 s plus a 3 s global cooldown. Distinct event IDs prevent replay. Use a short upper-body/additive or look-at pose, respecting the soldier's current locomotion.

`HardBlocked` is true for death, attack, block, stagger, rescue/carry contact, active tool strike, urgent player command, or any animation already owning the required slot. Call `UpdateReaction` while active; a new hard block cancels immediately. Clear the animation slot when ActiveKind becomes -1. Never issue movement/StopMovement from a reaction, and do not interrupt a cannon push/pull hand contact just to play a gesture.

## Fallen record candidate (#41)

One `BPC_FallenRecord41` per durable fallen record, owned by a persistent director. It is an isolated alternative to compare with the existing queue, **not an instruction to install two directors**. `RecordDeath` requires a real death ID, kind, wave, time and outside classification supplied from current castle bounds. It preserves the existing later-wave + 10-second eligibility rule; it does not assume the user's disappearance was intended.

`EvaluateRise` requires budget and valid navigation, with rescue only from confirmed commander rescue. When SpawnRequested first becomes true, reserve that FallenId and attempt one spawn. Maintain an in-flight actor registry; polling a true flag is not permission to spawn repeatedly. `ConfirmRise` with a null/failed actor keeps the record and retries after 1 second. A valid but unready/invisible actor stays pending for presentation recovery, avoiding duplicates. Only valid + presentation-ready confirmation permits the director to consume one record/budget unit. No callback/timeout should discard a body; reconcile or explicitly destroy a failed pending actor before reporting ActorValid=false and allowing another spawn.
