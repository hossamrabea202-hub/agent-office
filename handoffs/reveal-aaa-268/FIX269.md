# #269 fix: the 3D item is not drawn, and the reveal ends early (adjustedfilm, 2026-10-31 night)

Built on the Master's live test on card #269 (519-node graph in, satchel picked up, empty centre, ends after 1–2 s).

## What was wrong

**(a) Empty centre: the item never reached RT_Reveal.**
- I can't pin one cause without the editor, so the fix removes **every** way the item could drop out:
  1. **Alpha convention.** The capture wrote HDR colour with "inverse opacity" in alpha, and M_RevealItem trusted that.
     If 5.3 writes it the other way (or not at all), the item is cut out. Now the capture writes **scene depth** in alpha
     (`SCS_SceneColorSceneDepth`) and M_RevealItem keeps only pixels nearer than 30 m. That can't be inverted.
  2. **"Visible in scene capture only".** This flag (and Nanite, which handles it its own way) can keep a mesh out of a
     show-only capture. Both are now off for the two stage meshes. The stage is 2 km up and the meshes are hidden when
     no reveal plays, so nobody sees them in the sky.
  3. **Mobility.** A Static pivot or mesh can't be moved or spun at runtime. Every stage part is now Movable.
  4. **Show-only list.** It is set again at every reveal start, right after the item is made visible.
- **RvR 20** was not the problem. It is the item's size, and the capture distance follows it.
- **MPC_Reveal.Blur reading 0 in Python** is a read problem, not a game problem: the screen did blur.

**(b) "Ends after 1–2 s": not a frame timer.** The clock is real time (`GetRealTimeSeconds`). v2 did what card #268
asked ("~2 s"): the item left at 1.85 s and everything was gone by 3.1 s. The ~13 s you remember was the old v1 hint
card. **Decision:** the item now stays on screen until 3.2 s, flies to the dock at 3.2–3.6 s, the tree or pack opens at
3.6 s, and everything is gone by 4.45 s. You can still skip it with a click, Space, Enter, E or Esc.
