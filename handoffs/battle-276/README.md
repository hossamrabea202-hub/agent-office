## What it does

**By day** (BP_DayNight `IsNight` false): from day wave **4**, every **3rd** wave (4, 7, 10…):
- A **colossus** (`BP_ZombieWallBreaker`, #138) spawns 15 m outside a **random unbroken `BP_WallBreachSpot`**, with its `Target` set to that spot.
- **12 zombies** come with it.
- Notice: "A COLOSSUS approaches the *east* wall with its horde!" There are no siege engines by day.

**At dusk** the director picks **tonight's plan** at random and warns the player with the compass side (+X = north, +Y = east, the editor's convention):
- "Movement to the *west* of the castle… a siege is coming tonight."
- "Siege engines on two sides tonight, the first from the *west*…"
- "The horde gathers in the dark to the *west*…"

**Night waves from wave 6** run the plan with the #274 engines. Flank A = the director's spot − 15 m to its right; flank B = + 15 m.

| Plan | What happens |
|---|---|
| 0 **Feint** | 8 zombies storm from flank A at once. **20 s later** the real attack comes from flank B: a ram with 8 escorts. |
| 1 **Pincer** | A ram from flank A and, from wave **9**, a tower from flank B (a second ram before that), at the same time. Each engine has 8 escorts. |
| 2 **Hold & rush** | A ram (plus a tower from wave 9) leaves from the centre. **12 escorts hold** at flank A, out of crossbow range, with movement off. When an engine is within **25 m** of its target or breaks, **everyone charges**. |

- **Escorts** march at the engines' pace (130 cm/s, the same as the pushers).
- **They charge** (420 cm/s, movement on) when any engine is within 25 m of its gate or wall point, an engine breaks, or the hero comes within 15 m of an engine.
- **Defending the pushers:** zombies retarget to the nearest soldier, so escorts naturally turn on soldiers who come out.
- **Towers** are pointed at the nearest `SiegeWall` TargetPoint (as in #274).

**Decision (numbers, TGA):**
- **Colossus:** from day wave 4, every 3rd day wave, with a horde of 12.
- **Siege nights:** from night wave 6, towers from wave 9.
- **Feint:** 8 zombies, then the main attack 20 s later.
- **Escorts:** 8 per engine (12 when holding), march 130 cm/s, charge 420 cm/s, rush distance 25 m.
- **Flanks:** ±15 m from the director.

All are variables on BP_BattleDirector.

## Master steps

0. **Prerequisites:** #274's siege Blueprints set up and compiled (with the #251 art). #138's `BP_ZombieWallBreaker` and `BP_WallBreachSpot` must be in `/Game/CastleDefender/Gameplay/Siege/`; they are already in the project.
1. **Create the Blueprint:** run `py ".../handoffs/battle-276/setup_battle_ue.py"`. Set `DRY_RUN = True` first; the script also warns if a prerequisite is missing.
2. **Variables:** add the `VARIABLES.md` lines to BP_BattleDirector.
3. **Graph:** paste `BP_BattleDirector_graph.txt` into its EventGraph (delete the BeginPlay stub first), Refresh All Nodes and compile.
4. **Level:** place **one** BP_BattleDirector outside the walls on the zombies' road, inside the NavMesh, with its arrow pointing at the castle. Remove the BP_SiegeDirector if you placed it. Keep the **SiegeWall** TargetPoints from #274.
   - Flank A and flank B are 15 m to each side, so both must be on the NavMesh.
5. **Quick test:** set `ColossusFromWave` 1, `ColossusEvery` 1, `SiegeFromWave` 1 and `TowerFromWave` 1, then use the wave manager's ForceNextWave.

## Test list

1. **Day wave (test numbers):**
   - A colossus appears 15 m outside one breach spot and walks to it with 12 zombies.
   - The notice names its side.
   - Once a spot is broken it is no longer chosen.
2. **Dusk:** one warning notice with a compass side, once per night.
3. **Feint night:** 8 zombies run at the gate first. 20 s later a ram with 8 escorts comes from the other flank, and the escorts walk with it.
4. **Pincer night:** a ram and a tower (or 2 rams before wave 9) leave both flanks together. The tower heads for the nearest SiegeWall.
5. **Hold & rush night:**
   - 12 zombies stand still at flank A while the engines advance.
   - When the ram is 25 m from the gate (or you break it), they all charge.
   - Riding out to an engine (within 15 m) also triggers the charge.
6. **Escorts and soldiers:** escorts that meet soldiers fight them, using the normal zombie targeting.
7. **Day waves:** no engines.

## Notes and follow-ups

- **Breach-spot export:** I asked for the `BP_WallBreachSpot` export (request #280) to check that the spot does not also spawn colossi on its own. If it does, switch the spot's own spawning off, or set `ColossusEvery` very high and let the spot do it.
- **Escort speed:** escorts' speed is set on spawn and on the charge. If BP_Zombie resets `MaxWalkSpeed` in its own code (for example after a stagger), a marching escort may speed up early. That is harmless: it simply charges sooner.
- **Compass:** directions use world axes (+X north). If the map's "north" is different, tell me the rotation and I'll offset it.
