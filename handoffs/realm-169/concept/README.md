## What to read from them
- **The dome is huge.** It fills the sky behind the wall, much bigger than the wall itself. It is a thin **glassy membrane**: mostly clear, with a fine **vein / cell network** on it (like the tentacle jelly shells) and a bright rim where it curves away. It is **not** a solid purple blob.
- **Colour:** dusty rose to magenta in the membrane and veins, with the most saturation at the rim and in the glowing pods. The world outside stays natural: grey rock, green pines, brown wood, warm torches. The realm colour stays **inside** the dome.
- **Inside:** big tentacles rising, 2 giants with glowing eyes, zombies walking toward the gate, glowing pink egg pods on the ground, alien flora. Pink haze inside.
- **Wall:** a tall log palisade with sharpened tips. A double wooden **gate** with an arch, iron bands and lit torches. Square **watchtowers** with banners on both sides of the gate and along the wall. A dirt road with fences leading to the gate. A cart and a few rocks outside.
- **Night version:** a glowing pink **crack in the mountain** on the right, with the wasp swarm pouring out.
- **Wasps:** dark bodies, red-glowing abdomens, amber wings.
- **Mood:** epic and readable. Natural light outside, an alien glow inside.
