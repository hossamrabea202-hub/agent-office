# Tentacle ground eruption: cinematic spec (audit session, 2026-11-06, researched before execution)

Owner, after playing: "when i walked out suddenly this silly blocky appeared out of nowhere, no camera shake, no build up,
no tentacle rising from it, no dust, no particles, nothing. i want netflix cinematics animations and vfx the most
realistic. study online before executing."

## What is wrong today
- The #539 spore base is a 190 m static mesh placed in the level. World Partition streams its cell in when the hero
  walks out, so it pops into view. A rupture can never be a pre-placed static actor: it has to be a runtime event.
- Nothing announces it: no sound, no shake, no ground tell, no dust, no debris, and the tentacle is already up.
- The base itself renders as flat grey boxes (vertex colours not reaching the material): see
  `tentacle-spore-base-539/REVISION_1106.md`. That fix is a prerequisite, not part of this spec.

## What the references say (film)
- Dune / Dune: Prophecy (DNEG, Image Engine): the ground BULGES before it breaks, then breaks up, then dust. Separate
  behaviour for "submerged" (surface churn, the worm sign) and "breached". More effort went into the sand around the
  worm than the worm. Scale is sold by hiding part of the creature in dust and by low angles, not by showing it all.
- On set they buried a vibrating plate so actors react before anything is visible: the TELL is a separate beat.
- Creature method already in our KB (creature_studio_method.md): anticipation, a 2-3 frame strike, a hold, a slow
  recovery; motion = driver + damped followers; staccato holds are the Stranger Things creepiness.
- Game tutorials (CGHOW ground breaker, Chaos + Niagara, 80.lv rockfall): a character rising out of the ground is built
  from a debris fountain (mesh particles with collision, random scale/rotation), dust that rises then drifts, and a
  ground shockwave; Chaos fractures a mesh and its break events trigger the dust. Niagara Fluids for the dust cloud is
  the AAA route but is off the table on a 4 GB card.

## The sequence (one eruption, about 7 s, all runtime)
| T (s) | Beat | What the player sees / hears | Tools |
|---|---|---|---|
| -4.0 | TELL | Low rumble starts (sub bass + gravel), pebbles on the ground jitter, grass shivers, a faint ground bulge grows (10-30 cm), birds scatter. Soldiers bark "the ground!". Camera: low-amplitude, low-frequency shake | Niagara pebble emitter (mesh particles, 40-80), hidden DOME mesh under the landscape with World Position Offset driven by a material param 0 to 1, wind param kick on the WindDirectionalSource actors, UE Camera Shake (Perlin, loc amp 2-4 cm, rot 0.3 deg), sound cue with 4 s attack |
| -1.5 | HEAVE | Bulge jumps to 60-100 cm, dust vents from cracks, crack decal spreads outward from the centre (radial reveal), shake grows | Crack decal: dynamic material instance with a radial erosion mask param animated 0 to 1, 2-3 dust vent emitters at crack tips, shake amp x3 |
| 0.0 | BREACH | The crust bursts: 8-12 rock slabs thrown outward and up, a dense dust column with a ground shockwave ring, debris fountain, a hard camera hit (0.3 s, loc 15-25 cm, rot 2 deg, then 1.2 s decay), boom + bass drop, brief hit-stop (0.1 s global time dilation 0.3) | Spawn the spore base actor NOW (hidden until this frame; scale 0.85 to 1 over 0.4 s with a WPO heave), Chaos Geometry Collection of the slabs (clustered, anchored base, sleep in 3 s, then static), Niagara dust: lit sprites with soft depth, 200-400 particles, 2.5 s life, drift with wind; debris: 100-200 mesh particles with collision; shockwave: one ground-aligned ring sprite |
| 0.0 to 2.5 | RISE | The tentacle comes up through the dust: coiled, then uncoils with overshoot and a damped settle; sand and grit pour off it; the pods on the base split and puff spores | i5 clip RISE (driver + damped followers, 2-3 frame snap at the top, hold, slow settle), Niagara drip emitter on arm sockets (mesh grit, 1.5 s), spore puff emitter on the pods (sprites, slow drift, restrained emissive on tips only) |
| 2.5 to 7 | SETTLE | Dust thins and drifts downwind, slabs stop, last pebbles roll, rumble tail fades, spores keep drifting at low rate | Emitter loops end, Chaos pieces go static, sound tail 3 s |

Netflix framing, first eruption of a match only: a 4 s Sequencer shot, low angle from the hero's side, hero control
locked, HUD kept, slow push-in, the breach at the shot's 2.2 s so the cut back to gameplay lands on the rise. Later
eruptions play in-game with the same FX and shake, no camera takeover (the owner must not lose control twice).

## Realism rules (from the KB)
- Every layer has a physical cause: the bulge comes before the cracks, the cracks before the dust, dust before debris.
- Nothing glows except the spore tips (one restrained signature emissive); no magic rings, no stylised shockwave discs.
- Hide part of the arm in dust for the first second: scale is felt, not seen.
- Dust is lit by the sun and the sky (lit translucency, not unlit sprites), and drifts with the real wind direction.
- Sound leads picture: the rumble is audible 4 s before anything moves.

## Budget on the T1000 (4 GB) at 19.6 FPS baseline
- Dust + debris + pebbles together at most 1.5 ms GPU at V03, measured with stat gpu during the breach.
- Chaos: at most 12 slabs, one Geometry Collection, sleeps in 3 s, no field-driven propagation, no Niagara Fluids.
- The spore base stays Nanite but its material gets the vertex-colour fix first; the eruption never shows the base
  before the breach frame.
- No new post-process pass: shake only, no radial blur. The 0.1 s hit-stop costs nothing.
- Only one eruption at a time (queue them 6 s apart) so the dust never doubles.

## Who does what
- i5 (adjustedfilm/checkking): clip RISE (plus a RISE_FAST variant for later eruptions), DOME mesh with WPO material,
  slab source meshes for the Geometry Collection, crack decal textures (albedo + normal + radial mask), spore base
  vertex-colour fix (#539 revision).
- i9 Master (editor driver): Niagara systems (pebbles, dust vents, breach dust + shockwave, debris, drips, spore puff;
  Niagara cannot be authored by script, so hand-built from the engine templates), Camera Shake asset, Sequencer shot,
  Geometry Collection in Fracture Mode, and the BP wiring: BP_TentacleEruption (spawned by the wave manager at the
  tentacle's position, runs the timeline, spawns base + arm at T0), with --revert for every placed thing.
- Owner: picks the shake strength and the camera shot on a before/after, as usual.

## Order of work
1. #539 base material fix (i5), verified in UE by the Master.
2. BP_TentacleEruption timeline with placeholder FX (one dust emitter, shake, sound), spore base + arm spawned at T0.
   Play test: no pop-in, the rise completes, no arm indoors. This alone removes "appeared out of nowhere".
3. TELL and HEAVE layers (dome WPO, crack decal, pebbles, vents).
4. BREACH slabs (Chaos) + full dust/debris, budget measured.
5. RISE clip from i5 + drips + spore puff.
6. Sequencer first-eruption shot. Owner review.
